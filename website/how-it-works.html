How it works

From raw candles to a careful decision

Five steps run every minute, 24/7. The AI model is retrained every 12 hours and has to prove itself before it may trade.

  1. 01

    Market data

    Downloads OHLCV candles from any exchange supported by ccxt: Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken and more.

  2. 02

    Features

    Computes 16 indicators (returns, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, ATR, volume, EMA trend, volatility) using only past data.

  3. 03

    AI model

    A gradient-boosting classifier estimates the chance that price rises enough to beat fees over the next 6 candles.

  4. 04

    Edge check

    Walk-forward validation on unseen periods. No consistent edge over chance means no new trades.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Spot only, no leverage. Positions are sized by risk, with ATR stop-loss, take-profit and a trailing stop.

The difference

Sitting still is a feature

Most bots trade all the time and lose money through fees. GrgTrading has to prove itself first: before every use the model is tested on data it never saw. It is only allowed to trade if its buy signals beat the base rate overall and in most individual periods.

On a random-walk market, the model correctly refused to trade in 7 of 8 test runs.
