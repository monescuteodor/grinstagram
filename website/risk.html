Risk management

Built to lose small, not to gamble

Every trade and the account as a whole are protected by several independent limits.

Risk-based sizing

Each position is sized so that hitting the stop costs about 1% of equity, capped at 30% of the account.

Stops that follow price

ATR-based stop-loss and take-profit. The trailing stop moves up as price rises and locks in gains.

Daily loss pause

If the account drops more than 3–5% in a day, there are no new entries until the next UTC day.

Kill switch

A drawdown beyond the limit closes everything and stops the bot until you reset it yourself.

No withdrawal rights

The bot uses a trade-only API key. It can never move your money off the exchange.

Paper mode first

Starts with simulated money on real prices. Real money requires an explicit confirmation flag.
